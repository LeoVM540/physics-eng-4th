Surface/volume (cylinder) & $\dfrac{S}{V} = 2\left(\dfrac1r+\dfrac1h\right)$ & minimal at fixed $V$ for $h=2r$ \\

Cubic d-spacing & $d_{hkl} = \dfrac{a}{\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}}$ & also tetragonal/hexagonal forms in \cref{ch:xrd} \\
Bragg + cubic & $\sin^2\theta = \dfrac{\lambda^2}{4a^2}N$ & $N=h^2+k^2+l^2$; ratios of $\sin^2\theta$ = ratios of $N$ \\
Lattice parameter & $a = d\sqrt N = \dfrac{\lambda\sqrt N}{2\sin\theta}$ & average over peaks \\

Spin--orbit & $j = l \pm \tfrac12$, area ratio $(2j_1+1):(2j_2+1)$ & p 1:2, d 2:3, f 3:4; s single \\
XPS intensity & $I_i = N_i\sigma_i\lambda_iK$ & $N_A/N_B = (I_A/\sigma_A\lambda_A)/(I_B/\sigma_B\lambda_B)$ \\
Chemical shift & oxidation $\Rightarrow E_B\uparrow$ & electron gain $\Rightarrow E_B\downarrow$ \\
Mean particle size & $\bar d = \dfrac{\sum n_i d_i}{\sum n_i}$ & $\sigma = \sqrt{\sum n_i(d_i-\bar d)^2/\sum n_i}$ \\
STM current & $I_t\sim V\rho(E_F)e^{-2kd}$ & $k\approx\SI{1}{\per\angstrom}$: $\times10$ per \AA \\
AFM vs TEM & $\ell_\mathrm{cap}\approx(h_\mathrm{AFM}-d_\mathrm{TEM})/2$ & capped particle on flat support \\
Galvanic replacement & $E = E^0_\mathrm{red} - E^0_\mathrm{ox} > 0$ & spontaneous if positive \\
Microreactor & $t = \dfrac{\Delta d}{v}$, \ $V = Qt$, \ $L = \dfrac{V}{\pi r^2}$ & \SI{1}{\micro L} = \SI{1}{mm^3} \\
